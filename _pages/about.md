Haoran Yin is a PhD student at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies (UTIAS), where he is fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Steven Waslander](https://www.trailab.utias.utoronto.ca/steven-waslander) in the [Toronto Robotics and AI Laboratory (TRAIL)](https://www.trailab.utias.utoronto.ca/). He is also affiliated with the [University of Toronto Robotics Institute](https://robotics.utoronto.ca/) and the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/). His research is on trustworthy, learning-based robotics, with a focus on safety-critical control, robot manipulation, and embodied agents. His current work aims to make vision-language-action (VLA) policies safer and more reliable: teaching robots to recover from their failures and learn from them, and steering a policy's internal activations to prevent failures before they happen.

Before joining TRAIL, Haoran earned an M.S. in Robotics from Georgia Tech, where he was honored to be advised by [Dr. Glen Chou](https://glenchou.github.io/) in the [Trustworthy Robotics Lab](https://trustworthyrobotics.github.io/). There he worked on safety verification and data-driven reachability analysis for learned dynamics models and generative motion planners, and one of his papers was a **Best Paper Award finalist at L4DC 2026**. He also holds a B.A.Sc. in Computer Engineering, with a minor in Robotics and Mechatronics, from the University of Toronto.

If you are interested in Haoran's work or have any questions about his research, feel free to reach out at haoran[dot]yin[at]robotics[dot]utias[dot]utoronto[dot]ca. He is always happy to chat, and is open to research collaborations and internship opportunities.
